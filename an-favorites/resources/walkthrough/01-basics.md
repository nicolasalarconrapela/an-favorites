# Welcome to AnFavorites! 🌟

AnFavorites is a powerful extension that helps you manage and quickly access your favorite files and specific locations within your codebase.

## Key Features

### 📁 File Favorites

Mark any file as a favorite with a simple right-click from the editor, explorer, or editor tab context menu. Access your favorites instantly from the dedicated Favorites view in the Explorer sidebar.

### 📍 Line & Column Precision

Unlike simple bookmarks, AnFavorites remembers the **exact position** (line and column) where you added a favorite. When you open it, your cursor jumps right to that spot - perfect for tracking important code locations!

### 🎨 Visual Indicators

Favorites are visually indicated in your editor:

- **Gutter decorations**: See which lines are bookmarked
- **Context menu**: Right-click on the gutter to quickly add/remove favorites at specific lines

### 🔄 Multi-workspace Support

Working with multiple workspace folders? AnFavorites intelligently handles favorites across all your projects, with optional separation by workspace folder.

## Getting Started

Try adding your first favorite:

1. Open any file in your workspace
2. Right-click in the editor or on the gutter
3. Select **"AnFavorites: Add to Favorites"**
4. View it in the **Favorites** panel in the Explorer sidebar
