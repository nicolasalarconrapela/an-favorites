# Configure Your Shortcut ⌨️

AnFavorites comes with a default shortcut **Ctrl+Alt+F** to open **Quick Open** — your central hub for managing favorites.

## Customize Your Keybinding

You can change this shortcut at any time:

1. Press **Ctrl+K Ctrl+S** to open Keyboard Shortcuts
2. Search for `AnFavorites: Search File`
3. Set your preferred key combination

## Why This Matters

Quick Open is the fastest way to access your favorites, recent files, and search your workspace. A comfortable shortcut means you'll use it effortlessly throughout your workday.

## Pro Tip 💡

Choose a shortcut you can reach without looking — you'll be using it a lot!
