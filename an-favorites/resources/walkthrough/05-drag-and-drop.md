# Drag & Drop ✨

Reorder your favorites and groups effortlessly with full drag-and-drop support!

## What You Can Do

AnFavorites supports full drag-and-drop capabilities in the tree view:

- **Reorder favorites** within the same group by dragging them up or down
- **Move favorites between groups** by dragging them into a different group
- **Reorder groups** by dragging them to a new position

## How to Use

1. Go to the **AnFavorites** panel in the sidebar
2. **Click and hold** on a favorite or group
3. **Drag** it to the desired position
4. **Release** to drop it in place

## Multi-Select

Hold **Ctrl** (or **Cmd** on Mac) to select multiple favorites, then drag them all at once to a new group or position.

## Combining with Pins

Pinned favorites always stay at the top of their section. Combine drag & drop with pinning to create the perfect organization for your workflow.

## Pro Tip 💡

Use drag & drop to quickly reorganize when your project structure changes — no need to remove and re-add favorites!
