# Add Your First Favorite ⭐

There are several ways to add a file to your favorites — choose the one that fits your workflow!

## From the Editor

Right-click on a tab or inside the editor and select **"Add to Favorites"**.

## From the Explorer

Right-click any file in the file tree and select **"Add to Favorites"**.

## From Quick Open

1. Open Quick Open with your shortcut (default: **Ctrl+Alt+F**)
2. Search for the file you want
3. Click the **☆ star icon** on the right to toggle it as a favorite

## Pinning Favorites 📌

You can **pin** your most important favorites so they always appear at the top of Quick Open. Just click the pin icon next to any favorite in the tree view.

## Pro Tip 💡

Pinned favorites show up first in Quick Open, so pressing your shortcut + Enter opens your top pinned file instantly!
